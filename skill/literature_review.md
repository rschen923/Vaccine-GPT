# Literature review and synthesis

## Review design

Choose narrative, scoping, systematic, critical, or state-of-the-art review
before screening. For a broad VaccineGPT review, use a scoping/critical hybrid:
systematic discovery and transparent screening, followed by mechanistic
synthesis rather than unsupported meta-analysis.

## Evidence extraction

For each paper extract organism, strain, sample size, assay, perturbation,
controls, endpoint, effect size, uncertainty, validation set, code/data
availability, and limitations. Mark values as `reported`, `derived`, or
`not_available`; never infer a numerical result from a figure without labeling
the derivation.

## Synthesis

Organize each section as problem → method evolution → strongest evidence →
contradictions → current consensus → unresolved question. Compare studies by
design and measurement, not only by model name. Explicitly analyze dataset
leakage, homology overlap, batch effects, label quality, negative controls,
external validation, and biological plausibility.

## Quality gates

- PRISMA-style search and screening counts are recorded.
- At least one evidence table and one limitation table are included.
- Every substantive claim has a citation.
- Conflicting results and negative findings are discussed.
- Model metrics are only compared when splits and metrics are compatible.
- The conclusion distinguishes demonstrated results from proposals.
